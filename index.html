Aloha, my name is Emiri! (pronounced em-er-ee)

I’m a multidisciplinary creative combining social media strategy, design, and hands-on production to turn online attention into real-world action. My experience with nonprofits, close-knit agencies, and small businesses has made me a creative Swiss Army knife—adaptable, resourceful, and always looking for ways to work smarter and communicate better.

LinkedIn·Graphic Design Portfolio

The best stories begin with humility.

As a lifelong student at heart, I believe inspiration can come from just about anywhere, and every interaction is an opportunity to learn something new.

I’m naturally drawn to discovering what makes people unique. Once I find it, my mind goes straight to the possibilities: How can I bring this to light? That same instinct carries into teamwork, where I enjoy recognizing what each person brings to the table and finding smarter ways to make those strengths work together.

I approach every project with an open mind, thoughtful questions, and a genuine desire to make people feel comfortable and heard. Because I’ve found that the most compelling stories emerge when people feel free to be themselves.
